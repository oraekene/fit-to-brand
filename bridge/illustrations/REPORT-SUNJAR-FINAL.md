## What we built and for whom

SunJar SHS-200 is a 200 watt solar home system sold pay as you go: panel, battery
hub, four lamp points, two USB ports, and an app showing battery state and balance
[S0_SPEC]. It serves three buyers. Off grid mothers who want bedroom light without
kerosene smoke. Stall traders who want one more trading hour under light customers
trust. Field agents who earn per working hub and need arrears visibility before they
ride out [S5B_GROUPS].

## What the market looks like

Six situations mattered, ranked by what each decision moves and what ignorance costs
[S3_STAKES]. The stall hour moves about $60 a month and risks about $900 in a night
fire. Bedroom light moves about $14 in kerosene against about $180 in fire loss. The
school vote moves no household money, which the run flagged openly instead of hiding
[S-0006 price_note]. Six incumbents already serve these nights, from kerosene vendors
to the blue packed LuminaHome kit that owns the dealer shelf [S4_PRODUCTS]. White
space: nobody sells pay as you go light for evening classes. The graveyard holds dead
kerosene subscription pilots, which warned us off subscriptions as a model.

## Where we win and where we refuse

Bedroom lanterns swap out fully. Kiosk queues swap for home USB ports. The app reads
the mobile money receipt log for arrears checks. Classroom lanterns swap with
certified fitting. Cooling stays refused: a 200 watt hub cannot refrigerate, so no
cooling claim appears anywhere [S5A_FIT, NOTFIT]. Mains tied loads and grid tie stay
refused for the same reason. The objection we feared, that we look like LuminaHome,
ships with its answer: the amber rail, the 7 day trial, and packs the rival does not
have [Block B].
