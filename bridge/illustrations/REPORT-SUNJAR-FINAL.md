# SunJar final report (R-FINAL, standard depth)

## The ninth night

On the ninth night of the pilot, one of the five test homes left the kerosene
lantern unlit on the table and ate dinner under the hub lamp instead. Nobody logged
it as a milestone. The app did: four lamp points on, two phones charging, zero
kerosene [S0_SPEC acceptance]. SunJar SHS-200 is a 200 watt solar home system sold
pay as you go — panel, battery hub, four lamp points, two USB ports, and an app
showing battery state and balance. That dinner is what it is for.

## Who pays

Three buyers carry the launch. Off grid mothers buying bedroom light without smoke.
Stall traders buying one more hour under light their customers trust. Field agents
buying arrears visibility before they ride out [S5B_GROUPS]. Each brief ends in one
ask: a 7 day home trial, an order through the market agent before Friday, a Saturday
route briefing.

## The shelf

Six situations mattered. The stall hour moves about $60 a month and risks about $900
in a night fire. Bedroom light moves about $14 in kerosene against about $180 in
fire loss. The school vote moves no household money, flagged openly in the stakes
instead of hidden [S3_STAKES]. Six incumbents already serve these nights, and the
blue packed LuminaHome kit owns the dealer shelf [S4_PRODUCTS]. Nobody sells pay as
you go light for evening classes. Dead kerosene subscription pilots fill the
graveyard and warn us off subscriptions as a model.

## What we refuse

A 200 watt hub cannot refrigerate, so no cooling claim appears anywhere. Mains tied
loads stay out. Grid tie stays out. (The cooling row hurt to write. It would have
hurt more in a headline.) [S5A_FIT, NOTFIT] The objection we feared, that we look
like LuminaHome, ships with its answer attached: the amber rail, the trial, packs
the rival does not have [Block B].

## The system in plain words

One amber rail of paid light in a matte charcoal world. Archivo for display, Inter
for body. One rail device, dark space around it, label zones fixed on the pack face

silhouettes, no cooling claims, no invented type. Three scenes carry the launch: the
amber bedroom, the stall canopy at night, the rooftop install [THREADS].

## The name

screen, trademark clear, domain registered, handle held [NAMES]. AmberRail waits

GridFree promised more than the scope allows.
