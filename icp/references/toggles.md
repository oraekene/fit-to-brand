- T2 Tiering: `tier_decade` strict bands vs `tier_narrative` plus L-scale plus priceless T8 for non-market tails.
- T3 Situation style: `knowledge_need` record view vs `decision_moment` choice view, both stored per S-ID.
- T4 Product order: `View-Tier` prioritization vs `View-Domain` sales navigation, both derived from S4_PRODUCTS.csv.
- T5 Theme breadth: `theme_broad` TAM view vs `theme_sharp` Need-qualified view; runs are tagged, never silently mixed.
- T6 Segment wording: prosumer-concrete vs enterprise-formal briefs in S5B per GRP-ID (O-GTM).
- T7 Output overlay: O-GTM (ICPs + ad bodies) vs O-OPS (stakeholders + orders) vs O-EDU (cohorts + lesson briefs) vs O-SUBSISTENCE (staple-equivalent pricing) vs O-<custom>; same core tables, alternate group nouns, brief formats, channel enums and cert regimes per overlays.md.
